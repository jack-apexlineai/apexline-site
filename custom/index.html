What we build

Ambitious systems, built properly.

Custom code, Supabase, Google Workspace and automation, shaped around your business and connected to the tools you already use wherever they allow it.

Business operating systems

One system custom-coded around how your company runs: jobs, customers, staff, stock, documents and money in one place, with every rule and screen designed for the way your team works. It replaces the patchwork of tools and spreadsheets, and it grows with you.

  • Jobs and orders
  • Customers and accounts
  • Documents and records
  • Staff and permissions
  • Invoicing and payments

Logistics and fleet systems

Bookings, routing, driver apps, live job status and the documents that travel with every load. Built for domestic and international operations, with tracking and status updates your customers can see.

Dashboards of every kind

Live KPI dashboards, financial dashboards, staff performance and rankings, executive overviews, and automated reports that land in the right inbox on schedule. Pulled straight from your data, never retyped.

Customer and supplier portals

Secure logins where customers book, track and pay, and suppliers see orders, upload documents and update status. Web apps that work on any phone, with no app store needed.

Internal tools

Quoting engines that price a job from your own rules, job and workshop management, stock control, inspections and checklists. The tools your team uses all day, built exactly how they work.

AI agents and automation

AI agents that read, sort and draft: incoming emails, documents and requests handled and passed to the right place. Plus the automation that connects every part of the system, so information moves on its own instead of being copied by hand.

And everything around it

Lead capture and follow-up, enquiry routing and booking flows, built into the system rather than bolted on.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

How much does a build cost?

Every build is bespoke, so every price is too. Tell us what you want on a call or through the form and we'll send a proposal, with the price agreed before any work starts. Flexible payment options available.

What size of project do you take on?

Anything from a single internal tool to a full operating system for the business. On the discovery call we'll tell you straight what's realistic, and if something can't be done properly, we'll say so.

Can it connect to the tools we already use?

Usually, if the tool has a way in, such as an API, an export or a shared spreadsheet. We check every connection on the discovery call before it goes into a proposal.

What if it doesn't do what we agreed?

The proposal sets out exactly what the system has to do. You sign off only when it does all of it.

Who owns it?

Once it's paid for, the build is yours, and your data stays yours.

Do you look after it after launch?

Yes. We can host and maintain it for you with no long contract, or hand it over to your own team.
