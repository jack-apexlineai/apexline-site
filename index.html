FAQ

Questions people ask.

How much does a build cost?

Every build is bespoke, so every price is too. Tell us what you want on a call or through the form and we'll send a proposal, with the price agreed before any work starts. Flexible payment options available.

What size of project do you take on?

Anything from a single internal tool to a full operating system for the business. On the discovery call we'll tell you straight what's realistic, and if something can't be done properly, we'll say so.

Can it connect to the tools we already use?

Usually, if the tool has a way in, such as an API, an export or a shared spreadsheet. We check every connection on the discovery call before it goes into a proposal.

What if it doesn't do what we agreed?

The proposal sets out exactly what the system has to do. You sign off only when it does all of it.

Who owns it?

Once it's paid for, the build is yours, and your data stays yours.

Do you look after it after launch?

Yes. We can host and maintain it for you with no long contract, or hand it over to your own team.
