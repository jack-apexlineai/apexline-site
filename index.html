FAQ

Questions yards ask.

How long does it take to set up?

About 15 minutes on a call. We install it on your Google account, add your prices, lorries and customers, and you can take your first order the same day.

Where does my data live?

In your own Google account: your orders, customers and tickets sit in your Google Sheet and Drive. You can export it any time, and you keep it if you ever leave.

Do my drivers need to download an app?

No. They open a link on their phone and log in with their lorry registration and a PIN. It works on any smartphone.

What happens after the free trial?

You pick the plan that fits and pay monthly or yearly. If you're not happy within 14 days of your first payment, you get a full refund.

Can I mix and match features?

Yes. The plans cover most yards, but any feature can be switched on on its own. Tell us what you need on the call.

Does it help with digital waste tracking?

Yes. Every load received is logged with its EWC code, weights and carrier, and WasteLine fills in the Defra spreadsheet ready for you to upload.
