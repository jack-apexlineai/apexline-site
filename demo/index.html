What drivers can do
  1. See today's jobs as runs: what to load before leaving the yard (up to 3 empties), and when to come back and tip.
  2. Press Start on a job: the office sees it on Live tracking, and the customer gets an "on the way" email or text if the order was ticked.
  3. Do the jobs in any order, put a job back, report a problem, or add photos to a job. Photos file in that order's Drive folder.
  4. Get the customer to sign on the phone. The waste transfer note is made and filed in Drive automatically.
  5. Log breaks and tip runs, then clock out. Clocked out by mistake? Clock straight back in.
